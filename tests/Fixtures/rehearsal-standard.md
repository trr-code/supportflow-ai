# Meridian Civic Theater — rehearsal access standard
Revision 3 · Effective October 1, 2026 · Fictional test document

## Scope and precedence
Revision 3 replaces Revision 2 for bookings on or after October 1, 2026. A signed event addendum overrides this standard only for the specific items it names. The amounts below are in USD.

## Deposits and deadlines
A standard rehearsal requires a refundable $180 deposit. Finance must receive it by 4:30 p.m. Central on the second business day before the booking. The equipment hold is a separate $60 authorization. It is released within three business days after equipment is returned. An inspection happens before the $180 deposit is refunded.

## Cancellation
Cancel at least 72 hours before scheduled access for a full deposit refund. With less than 72 hours, the theater keeps $45 and refunds the rest. If the theater closes the building, the full deposit is refunded regardless of notice. Equipment damage is assessed separately.

## Door access and late arrivals
The east service door is staffed until 8:15 p.m. Central. After that, use the south intercom and state the booking ID. The duty lead must approve after-hours entry. A rehearsal booking alone does not authorize entry.

## Accessibility
The south entrance has a step-free route. The east service door has a 4-inch threshold. The lift booking is separate from the rehearsal booking. Its operating hours appear on the event sheet.
