NORTHSTAR STUDIO — TEAM HANDBOOK
Fictional sample document for the Lucid demo. These policies do not describe a real employer.

## REMOTE WORK
Team members may work remotely up to three days each week. Agree on the schedule with your manager. Core collaboration hours are 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday.

## EQUIPMENT
New team members receive a company laptop and a one-time home office allowance of $400. Get manager approval before buying equipment. Submit itemized receipts within 30 days of purchase. The allowance does not cover personal phone plans.

## TIME OFF
Full-time team members receive 20 days of paid time off each calendar year. Submit requests at least 10 business days ahead when possible. Your manager approves requests based on team coverage. Up to five unused days may carry into the next calendar year and must be used by March 31.

## LEARNING
Each team member has an annual learning budget of $750 for approved courses, books, and professional events. Ask your manager before enrolling. Unused learning funds do not carry forward.

## TRAVEL
Business travel needs manager approval before booking. The meal allowance is $60 per travel day, excluding alcohol. Submit receipts within 14 days after returning. This handbook does not specify mileage reimbursement rates or international travel rules; ask the operations team for those details.
